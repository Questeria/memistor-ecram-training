\subsection{Fidelity of the aggregate update}\label{sec:fidelity}
The simulator draws each batch's pulses as independent Poisson counts per cell and applies them in one exact step of the
interleaved dynamics (Section~\ref{sec:model}). A physical stochastic-pulse array instead fires row and column lines per slot
and per sample; a cell is pulsed when both of its lines fire, and the samples follow one another. After the second review we
compared the two, pre-registered with pass lines (\texttt{PLAN-REVISION-2.md}, experiment A), on the operating regime of the
main table.

\paragraph{Update level (A1).} At two points of training, one batch's inputs, errors and array state were taken from surrogate
training runs and applied repeatedly from the same state by three methods: the surrogate; the explicit stream applied pulse by
pulse (Bernoulli line firings, the per-pulse device step, write noise and bounds after every pulse); and a per-sample form of the
same stream (each sample's same-sign train in closed form, samples in order), which replays the literal stream's firings. The
cases cover clipped and unclipped layers: MNIST Li-ECRAM c-TTv2 (first and output layer), CIFAR MO-ECRAM AGAD (fully connected and
output layer; the worst-clipped layer of the main table) and the LM protonic-inspired c-TTv2 (embedding and output layer),
\RXProbeN{} probes (Table~\ref{tab:rev2_probe}).
\begin{itemize}
\item The surrogate meets the pre-registered lines (realised gain along the requested update within 10\,\%, mean difference
      beyond sampling noise at most 0.10, median variance ratio in $[0.5, 2]$) on \RXProbeSurPassN{} of \RXProbeN{} probes,
      including layers that lose up to \RXProbeOkLostMax\,\% of the requested pulse mass to clipping. Its per-cell variance
      ratio to the literal stream lies between \RXProbeSurVarMin{} and \RXProbeSurVarMax.
\item It fails on the two probes of the most clipped layer: \RXProbeSurFailList. There almost nothing of the requested update
      survives the clipping, and the surrogate's Poisson counts, which can exceed the slot budget that caps the stream, no longer
      match it (review 2's limiting case: Poisson(31) exceeds 31 with probability 0.4524).
\item The per-sample stream matches the literal one on all \RXProbeSeqPassN{} probes (gain within \RXProbeSeqGainDevMax\,\%,
      variance ratio \RXProbeSeqVarMin--\RXProbeSeqVarMax), so it is a valid reference for training.
\item The cross-cell correlation that shared lines introduce is small in these cases: the mean residual correlation among cells
      sharing a row or a column is at most \RXProbeSlotRhoMaxAll{} in absolute value.
\end{itemize}
The probes draw from a generator of their own: \RXProbeRunsEqualEThree{} of the \RXProbeRunsN{} probe runs (MNIST, CIFAR)
reproduce their main-table records exactly. The LM run does not, because LM training on the GPU is not bitwise reproducible:
configurations repeated exactly in the records differ by up to \RXReproLmMaxPct\,\% in bits per byte (CIFAR
\RXReproCifarMaxPct\,\%).

\paragraph{Training level (A2).} The same selections, learning rates and seeds as the main table were trained with the
per-sample stream (MNIST: every analog layer, three devices $\times$ SGD, c-TTv2, AGAD; CIFAR: the two fully connected layers,
whose rows are one per image, for Li-ECRAM and protonic-inspired c-TTv2 and MO-ECRAM AGAD; the convolutional layers keep the
surrogate), seeds 0--2, and compared seed by seed with the main table's surrogate runs (Table~\ref{tab:rev2_fid}). The
pre-registered rule calls the change material if any cell's mean gap moves by more than half the line (1\,pp) or two cells
whose gaps differ by more than 1\,pp swap order.
\begin{itemize}
\item By that rule the result is \textbf{material}, through one cell: \RXFidMaterialList, whose gap moves from
      \RXFidMnistEcramMoKimTwoZeroOneNineSgdSur{} to \RXFidMnistEcramMoKimTwoZeroOneNineSgdSeq\,pp
      (difference \RXFidMnistEcramMoKimTwoZeroOneNineSgdD\,pp, 95\,\% interval [\RXFidMnistEcramMoKimTwoZeroOneNineSgdLo,
      \RXFidMnistEcramMoKimTwoZeroOneNineSgdHi]). It is a failing cell, far over the line under either update, and the interval
      includes zero; we report it as not validated against the explicit stream.
\item No pair of cells swaps order (\RXFidSwapsN{} swaps), and no Tiki-Taka-family cell moves by more than
      \RXFidTtMaxAbs\,pp (MNIST at most \RXFidMnistMaxAbs\,pp including the SGD cell; CIFAR at most \RXFidCifarMaxAbs\,pp, with
      intervals of several pp at three seeds).
\end{itemize}
Within these bounds (MNIST on every layer, CIFAR on its fully connected layers, the LM at update level only, three seeds), the
aggregate surrogate preserves the ranking and the pass/fail reading of the tested Tiki-Taka-family cells. It is not validated
where clipping removes nearly all of the update (the CIFAR output layer above), for the convolutional layers, or for the LM's
training trajectory, and the main results remain comparisons within this aggregate-update simulator.
